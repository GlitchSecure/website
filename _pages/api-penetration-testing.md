## Test the API behind your SaaS product

APIs concentrate sensitive data and product logic behind endpoints that are easy to expose and difficult to test well. GlitchSecure combines manual penetration testing with purpose-built tooling to assess REST, GraphQL, and other HTTP-based APIs in the context of the product they support.

Our testers work from your documentation, available roles, and representative test accounts. We map the exposed attack surface, examine how identities and tenants are separated, and test whether an attacker can reach data or actions beyond their intended permissions.

## What an API penetration test covers

- Object- and function-level authorization, including cross-tenant access
- Authentication flows, session handling, API keys, JWTs, and OAuth integrations
- Input handling, injection risks, unsafe deserialization, and mass assignment
- Rate limits, resource consumption, and abuse of sensitive workflows
- REST and GraphQL endpoints, including undocumented or legacy routes discovered during testing
- Business-logic weaknesses that require manual exploration rather than scanner signatures

Testing is aligned with recognized OWASP application-security guidance while remaining specific to your architecture and risk model. Scope, environments, roles, and exclusions are agreed before testing begins.

## Findings your developers can act on

Verified findings appear in the GlitchSecure platform as they are discovered, so your team can begin remediation before the final report. Each issue includes evidence, risk context, and practical remediation guidance. Once a fix is ready, you can request retesting and keep the report current.
