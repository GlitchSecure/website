Penetration Testing as a Service (PTaaS) companies rely heavily on outsourcing to pools of freelancers, most of whom are juggling multiple gigs at any given moment. GlitchSecure is a better solution for businesses who want focussed, personalised security testing performed by experts.

---

## PTaaS relies on volume, GlitchSecure prioritises quality

As a collective of hackers, GlitchSecure is a dedicated group of experts who will never outsource your security to gig workers. The experience and care taken by our team delivers a level of quality that overburdened pools of freelance testers simply can’t match.  

---

## GlitchSecure learns your platform, goes beyond the checklist

To effectively test your platform, we first take the time to understand how it works. Rather than strictly relying on generic checklists, our team collaborates on a personalised threat model based on known risks for your business. A crowd-sourced PTaaS vendor won’t devote any more time than the bare minimum when evaluating your platform.


---

## GlitchSecure is a partner, not a vendor


[Our customers think of GlitchSecure as an extension to their own teams](https://glitchsecure.com/about/#reviews). We’re not in the business of selling mass-produced PDFs, and don’t partner with companies who are only interested in ticking a certification box for bare minimum security. We invest in your success with a [collaborative partnership that goes beyond the terms of a contract](https://www.capterra.com/p/10008516/GlitchSecure/reviews/6166596/).

---

## GlitchSecure goes above and beyond typical PTaaS on skills, service, and expertise

Gain peace of mind from the decades of expertise we bring, knowing we’ve got your back long after the pentest is over. Unlike casual PTaaS vendors, GlitchSecure gives your team expert remediation advice, re-testing, and security guidance for a full year.

---

## GlitchSecure is for SaaS, by SaaS

As a SaaS company ourselves, we understand the complexities inherent in application development and know the importance of getting software security right. Why trust your platform to a vendor who has no experience in developing their own?
