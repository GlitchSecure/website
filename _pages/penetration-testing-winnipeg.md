## Local expertise for Canadian software teams

GlitchSecure is a Winnipeg-based cybersecurity company helping SaaS and software teams identify and remediate vulnerabilities. Our penetration tests combine hands-on testing by experienced hackers with a collaborative platform for real-time findings, remediation guidance, and retesting.

We work remotely with organizations across Canada and can support Winnipeg and Manitoba teams that value a local security partner. Our office is at 120 Sherbrook Street in Winnipeg.

## Penetration testing services

- SaaS and web application penetration testing
- [API penetration testing](/api-penetration-testing/) for REST, GraphQL, authentication, authorization, and business logic
- External and internal network penetration testing
- Testing support for security and compliance programs
- Remediation testing and an always-current final report

Every engagement begins with a clear scope. We review the applications, environments, user roles, integrations, and higher-risk workflows that need coverage, then tailor the assessment to the product rather than treating it as a generic scan.

## Manual testing with real-time results

Automated tools are useful for coverage, but they do not understand tenant boundaries, permission models, or how a SaaS workflow should behave. Our testers manually investigate those areas and validate findings before they reach your team.

Results are delivered in the GlitchSecure platform as they are confirmed. Developers can review evidence, ask questions, track remediation, and request a retest without waiting for the end of the engagement. An always-current PDF report remains available for customers and compliance stakeholders.

If you need ongoing testing between annual assessments, our [continuous security testing platform](/platform/) and [managed DAST service](/continuous-security-testing/) provide recurring coverage with expert verification.
